# Ai Governance

## Procedure

Map use cases, data sensitivity, impact, oversight, tools, retention, access, auditability, and incident response. Classify risks, assign owners, and produce a lightweight policy and control checklist. Flag legal questions.

## Output

Return the structured artifact, assumptions, unresolved questions, and concrete verification or review criteria. Communicate with the user in Traditional Chinese unless another language is requested.
