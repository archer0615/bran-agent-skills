# Project Tracking

## Procedure

Collect milestones, progress, blockers, decisions, dependencies, owners, dates, and signals. Classify status as on track, at risk, or blocked. Produce summary, actions, risks, and next checkpoint without inventing status.

## Output

Return the structured artifact, assumptions, unresolved questions, and concrete verification or review criteria. Communicate with the user in Traditional Chinese unless another language is requested.
